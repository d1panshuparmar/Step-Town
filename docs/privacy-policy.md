# Stepwize Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-09

Stepwize (“the App”) is a walking and town-building game.

## Data we collect

- **Account data:** email address (for sign-in via Supabase).
- **Game progress:** town state, inventory, steps converted to in-game coins (synced to your account when cloud save is enabled).
- **Motion / activity data:** step counts from the device pedometer / activity APIs, used only to award in-game currency. We do not sell this data.

## How we use data

- Authenticate you and restore your town
- Convert steps into Step Coins inside the game
- Improve reliability of cloud saves

## Storage

Account and cloud saves are stored with Supabase. On-device cache may also store progress locally.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Providers (e.g. Supabase) process data only to run the App.

## Your choices

You may sign out, request account deletion by contacting the developer, or revoke motion permission in system settings (step earning will stop).
